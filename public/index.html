Frequently Asked Questions

Do my customers need to install an app from App Store or Play Store?

No, absolutely not! When your customer scans the QR code with their regular smartphone camera, the loyalty pass and scratch card open directly inside their native mobile browser in 1.2 seconds. This removes 100% of download friction.

How does the 1 Device = 1 User Anti-Cheat protection work?

Our system runs a client-side hardware fingerprint (combining screen metrics, WebGL GPU signature, canvas hash, and persistent storage token). Once a physical phone registers a phone number, it is locked to that account. Nobody can enter 10 fake phone numbers on the same phone to exploit rewards!

Can I print QR standees for Table 1 to 20 or the Billing Counter?

Yes! Inside your Admin Dashboard, there is a built-in Table QR Standee Station. Simply select Table 1, Table 2, or Billing Counter, and click "Print Table Standee". It outputs a beautiful print-ready tabletop tent card formatted for acrylic stands.

How does the Negative Review Firewall protect my Google Maps score?

If a customer taps 4 or 5 stars, the AI generates positive praise and directs them to post on Google Maps. If they select 1, 2, or 3 stars, the system displays an optional Private Manager Resolution Box so you resolve their issue privately at the table before they leave an angry public review!
